Travel tips

Getting there

  • Driving

    Put CF82 7SN into your sat nav. The hotel sits just off the A472 between Ystrad Mynach and Maesycwmmer, around 20–30 minutes from Cardiff or Newport. Leave a little extra time on a Saturday afternoon.

  • Parking

    The hotel has its own car park for guests, so there's no need to find street parking. Car sharing is a good idea if you're coming from the same way as others in the group.

  • By train

    The nearest stations are Ystrad Mynach and Hengoed, both on the Rhymney line from Cardiff Queen Street / Cardiff Central. From either station it's a short taxi ride to the hotel. Check Transport for Wales times.

  • Taxis

    Book your taxi home in advance. Saturday evenings get busy and 5:30 PM comes round quickly once the cake is out.
